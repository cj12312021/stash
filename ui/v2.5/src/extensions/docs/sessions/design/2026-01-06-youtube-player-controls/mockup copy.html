Stash Player Design (YouTube 2026 Layout)

Progress Bar

Thin 3px bar at bottom, expands to 5px on hover. Stash primary #137cbd played, gray buffered. Circular scrubber appears on hover only.

Control Layout

Left: Play, Volume+slider, Time, Chapter. Right: Grouped container (Autoplay, CC, Settings, Fullscreen).

Autoplay Switch (PR #6368)

Form.Switch style toggle. Pill track with sliding thumb. ON: thumb RIGHT, white bg + play ▶ icon. OFF: thumb LEFT, dark bg + pause ⏸ icon.

Right Panel Grouping

Right buttons grouped in dark pill container rgba(32,43,51,0.9) (Stash body-bg) with border-radius: 8px. Smaller buttons inside.

Time Display

Format: 0:00 / 20:48 - white text, slash separator. Uses Roboto font at 12px.

Volume Slider

Inline horizontal slider that expands from volume button on hover. Width 52px, white fill.

Button Style

White filled SVG icons 22px. Subtle rgba(255,255,255,0.1) background on hover.

Transparent Background

Updated 2026: YouTube no longer uses a gradient. Controls float on transparent background. Controls hide when playing.

Point Markers

Cyan #00d4ff vertical tick marks for single-time markers. 3px wide, 14px tall, expands to 18px on hover with glow effect.

Range Markers

Amber rgba(255,170,0,0.35) overlay segments for time ranges. 2px solid borders, brightens to 0.5 opacity on hover.

Chapter Indicator

Shows current marker title. Click to open dropdown with all markers. Cyan dots for point markers, amber for ranges. Active marker highlighted.

Settings Menu (YouTube 2026)

Toggles first (Loop, Auto-start, Scrubber), then submenus (Quality, Speed, Subtitles, Filters, Transforms). Custom speed slider with fine-grained control. HD/4K badges on quality options.

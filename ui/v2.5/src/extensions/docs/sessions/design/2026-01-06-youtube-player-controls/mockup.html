YouTube 2024 Design Features

Progress Bar

Thin 3px bar at bottom, expands to 5px on hover. Red #f00 played, gray buffered. Circular scrubber appears on hover only.

Control Layout

Left: Play, Volume+slider, Time, Chapter. Right: Autoplay, CC, Settings, Miniplayer, Theater, Fullscreen.

Time Display

Format: 0:00 / 20:48 - white text, slash separator. Uses Roboto font at 12px.

Volume Slider

Inline horizontal slider that expands from volume button on hover. Width 52px, white fill.

Button Style

White filled SVG icons 22px. Subtle rgba(255,255,255,0.1) background on hover.

Gradient Background

Dark gradient from rgba(0,0,0,0.9) at bottom fading to transparent. Controls hide when playing.
